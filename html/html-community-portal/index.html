Community Bulletin

The annual Community Festival is just around the corner! Join us for a weekend of fun, food, and entertainment. This year we have expanded our venue to accommodate more stalls and activities. Local artists will showcase their work, and there will be live music throughout the event.

Volunteers are needed for setup and coordination. If you can spare a few hours, please sign up at the community center. We also welcome sponsorship from local businesses. Your support helps keep this event free for all residents. Let us come together to celebrate our vibrant community!
